# Surface observations and finishing experiments

GLB inspection now records actual unique-mesh triangle/shading observations in `surface_diagnostics`. The same artifact-scoped metadata appears under a collapsed section in Library, Production and Compare. Existing artifacts without a report do not display invented measurements. Original files, review state, QA state and publication remain unchanged.

For each unique local mesh, measurements include zero-area triangles, very thin triangles, the first percentile of triangle shape quality, finite vertex normals, source-space surface area and faces whose geometric normal opposes the mean stored vertex normal. Triangle shape quality is `2*sqrt(3)*double_area / sum(edge_length_squared)`, so an equilateral triangle scores one and a collapsed triangle zero. The reported thin count uses `<0.001`. This threshold describes shape; it does not declare an asset artistically invalid. Processing uses bounded face chunks instead of duplicating the whole triangle array at once.

The installed Trimesh GLB loader retains explicit NORMAL accessors as cached vertex normals; reports distinguish loaded versus computed normals. Opposed mean normals can indicate shading issues but can also arise from intentional custom/smooth normals. These counts do not prove holes, self-intersections, UV overlap, silhouette quality, PBR fidelity or artistic acceptance. Nonfinite normals are a technical inspection error. Ordinary observation counts do not silently change technical/artistic approval policies.

Two actual generated assets were used to research remesh projection from the same native checkpoint/PBR. Full projection reduced sampled surface distance on the retained tower and complex castle. However, the castle also developed visible speckled/kinked regions. Measured thin triangles increased from 1 to 113 and opposing-normal faces from 2,042 to 6,046. Half projection reduced those counts to 7 and 3,062, still above baseline. No projection change is enabled by default, no installed engine is edited, and no source or previously delivered asset is replaced. Surface-distance improvement alone is insufficient evidence to adopt a finishing change.

`scripts/verify_finishing_research.py` provides an explicit isolated reproduction lane using retained workspace/.local checkpoint data, the existing GPU/host RAM locks and a copied worker. It is not a product feature or a promise of a better model. It records the delta, source/config/worker hashes and actual technical inspection. Do not claim mid-diffusion resume or a new artistic score from these runs.

Tests use actual GLBs with deliberately reversed normals, a collapsed triangle, a thin triangle and shared instances at different scales. These are negative geometry fixtures, not fabricated successful AI outputs.
