# Asset Forge Studio — Single prompt examples

A Single prompt describes ONE asset. Write in English or Vietnamese. The more of the
attributes below you state, the closer the result; anything you leave out is decided
by AI (or asked, when "Allow questions" is on).

## Attribute checklist (most important first)

| Attribute | Why it matters | Example words |
|---|---|---|
| Subject + variant | Sets the silhouette | western dragon with four legs and wings; wyvern; oriental dragon |
| Role / size class | Sets scale and detail | boss, elite enemy, common unit, small prop, landmark |
| Main colours | Requested colours stay dominant | crimson red with gold horns; navy and silver |
| Materials | Surface look | weathered oak, wrought iron, polished marble, scaled hide |
| Condition / age | Wear and damage | brand new, battle-worn, ancient ruined, mossy |
| Era / culture | Shape language | medieval European, fantasy elven, Nordic, desert |
| Distinctive parts | What makes it unique | glowing rune eyes, broken left horn, banner on the roof |
| Size in metres | Exact real-world size (or use Size & scale) | 18 m tall; 1.1 m tall |
| Held items (characters) | Weapons and shields become separate socket-tagged assets | swordsman with a round shield |

Characters and creatures are generated rig-ready (A-pose) automatically; mention a pose
only if you need a static statue. Do NOT describe camera angle, background, lighting,
polycount or file format: framing, clean background and export are handled by the app.

## Complete examples

1. Building:
   Archer watchtower for a fantasy kingdom: square stone base 5 m wide, timber upper platform with crenellated wooden railing, red cloth roof, a small banner on top, weathered grey stone and warm oak, 10 m tall.
2. Character (rig-ready):
   Elite knight commander, full plate armour in polished silver with royal blue tabard and gold trim, plumed helmet, cape, holding a longsword and a kite shield with a lion crest, 2 m tall.
3. Creature boss:
   Giant frost wyvern boss, pale ice-blue scales with dark navy spines, crystalline ice shards growing from its back and tail, torn membrane wings, glowing white eyes, about 10 m long.
4. Prop:
   Weathered oak barrel with three wrought-iron hoops and a wooden lid, slightly dented, warm brown wood, 1.1 m tall.
5. With a style reference image (attach the image):
   một con rồng boss đỏ khổng lồ, phù hợp với các công trình này
   The image gives colours, materials and rendering style only, never its shape.

## Other Single options in Create

- Output: 3D model or 2D sprite.
- Quality: Fast / Balanced / Maximum (Quality Lock never lowers Maximum silently).
- Seeds: 1–5 distinct numbers = number of 3D candidates compared.
- Size & scale: preview next to reference objects; a confirmed size is used exactly.
- Allow questions: off = AI decides missing details; on = up to 10 questions first.
